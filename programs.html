Academic Strands & Programs

Four strands, one mission: preparing every student for college, career, or entrepreneurship.

📊

STEM — Science, Technology, Engineering & Mathematics

Built for students eyeing engineering, medicine, computer science, and the natural sciences. Heavy on laboratory work, research, and advanced mathematics.

Research-Based Lab Immersion Pre-Med / Pre-Engineering
💰

ABM — Accountancy, Business & Management

Covers financial literacy, entrepreneurship, marketing, and organizational management for future business leaders and accountants.

Mini-Enterprise Projects Bookkeeping Leadership
🎤

HUMSS — Humanities & Social Sciences

For students drawn to law, education, communication, psychology, and the social sciences, with strong writing and critical discourse training.

Public Speaking Creative Writing Community Immersion
🛠

TVL — Technical-Vocational-Livelihood

Hands-on training in ICT, Home Economics, and Industrial Arts specializations, with NC II certification pathways for immediate employability.

NC II Certification Work Immersion Skills-Based
Enrollment Journey

How to enroll in SHS

Step 1

Submit requirements

Report card, certificate of good moral character, and a completed application form.

Step 2

Strand assessment & interview

A short guidance interview helps confirm the strand that best fits the student's goals.

Step 3

Confirm & pay

Settle enrollment fees at the cashier or through the online payment portal.

Step 4

Section assignment

Receive your official section and advisory teacher before the first day of classes.
