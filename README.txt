LOURDES COLLEGE SENIOR HIGH SCHOOL — GROUP 1

START HERE
1. Extract this ZIP into one folder.
2. Open index.html in your browser.
3. Keep every file together. The website works without installation.

SIX INTERCONNECTED PAGES
index.html       Home: school logo, header, welcome, department introduction,
                 mission, vision, core values, featured image, and the required footer.
about.html       Department introduction and school values.
programs.html    Academic pathways and TVL, with illustrative study areas.
faculty.html     Principal, faculty, discipline, ITCs, advisers, and support.
activities.html  Areas of student participation and the official news link.
contact.html     School contact details, published office hours, and FAQs.

OTHER FILES
style.css        Shared colors, layout, and mobile styles.
script.js        Mobile navigation and copyright year.
school-logo.png  Authentic Lourdes College seal.
campus.jpg       Chapel and auditorium photograph, 2008.
featured.jpg     Entrance gate photograph, 2008.
CREDITS.txt      Sources, image attribution, and licenses.

BEFORE SUBMITTING
- In faculty.html, replace each "Name to be confirmed" with a verified name.
- Add approved staff photographs or group members if your teacher asks for them.
- Mission and vision are labeled summaries, with a link to the official text.
  If exact institutional wording is required, use text supplied by your teacher.
- Program study areas and possible pathways are examples, not a current prospectus.
- Activities are general participation areas, not an announced event calendar.
- The two campus photos are historical and labeled 2008. Replace them with your
  own current school photos if preferred and update captions and image credits.
- The contact information was checked on October 2, 2026. Reconfirm before use.

EDITING GUIDE
Change page content directly in each HTML file.
Change the theme colors at the top of style.css.
The same navigation and footer are included in every page so the site works
when opened directly from a folder. Update all six if you change their links.

No frameworks, package downloads, accounts, or backend are required.
Local pages and images work offline. Official external links require internet.
This is a student project, not an official school service.
