About the department

Preparing for life,
one lesson at a time.

Senior High School is a time to explore interests, practice independence, and make thoughtful choices about the future. Grades 11 and 12 help students build on their earlier learning and develop a clearer direction.

Lourdes College’s SHS program combines preparation for further education with the school’s commitment to personal growth and service.
