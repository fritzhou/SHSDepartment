Our commitment

Mission

Lourdes College commits to developing people who put their faith into action, pursue excellent work, and serve with humility.

  • Deepen spiritual reflection and responsible freedom.
  • Prepare compassionate leaders for different communities.
  • Improve learning through fresh ideas and approaches.
  • Develop the capacity to address present-day needs.
  • Extend educational opportunities to people experiencing poverty.
Our shared direction

Vision

An Ignacian Marian community inspired by Jesus’ compassion, forming humble learners who can lead in a global setting, stand with others, and help build a better society.
